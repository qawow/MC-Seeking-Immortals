# 2026-07-02 0.1.71 Alchemy three-part furnace

## Summary

- Upgraded the alchemy furnace MVP so refining requires an installed lid, dan fire, and matching formula.
- Added tiered lid/fire component items and formula carrier items for the current MVP pill recipes.
- Bumped mod_version to 0.1.71; ModNetwork.PROTOCOL_VERSION remains 6.

## Gameplay

- The existing alchemy furnace is treated as tier 1.
- Furnace block entities persist lidTier, fireTier, knownFormulaId, and formulaSource.
- Fire above furnace tier explodes and destroys the furnace.
- Fire above lid tier blows the lid and damages nearby players.
- Furnace tier mismatch or insufficient realm control wastes half ingredients and half mana.
- Foundation Building Pill is controlled: wild paper formulas are weaker than sect-secret formulas.
- Success chance now factors furnace tier, fire tier, alchemy skill, material rarity, realm control, formula source, and leyline aura.
